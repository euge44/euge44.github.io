## About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Georgia Tech, advised by [Prof. Wenke Lee](https://wenke.gtisc.gatech.edu/).

My research interests lie broadly in AI security and privacy, with a particular focus on the security of LLM agents and adversarial machine learning.

Previously, I completed an M.S. in Computer Science at Georgia Tech and an engineering degree at IMT Atlantique in France. I also worked as an AI Security Research Intern at Thales cortAIx Labs.
